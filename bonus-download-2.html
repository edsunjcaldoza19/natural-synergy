Need help opening your files?

90% of computers will automatically open all the download files. If your computer fails to open one of the files, follow the instructions below.

All of our ebooks are .pdf format. To open .pdf files, use Adobe Reader software. It’s free at http://get.adobe.com/reader/

All our audio files are .mp3 format. Most computers will have good mp3 audio software already. If an .mp3 file doesn’t open, get Apple Itunes for free at https://www.apple.com/itunes/

All video files are .mp4 format. If an .mp4 file doesn’t open on your computer, get VLC Player for free at http://www.videolan.org/vlc/index.html

Some large files have been compressed as .zip files. This allows you to download them faster. If your computer doesn’t extract these files by itself, get PeaZip for free at http://peazip.sourceforge.net/
